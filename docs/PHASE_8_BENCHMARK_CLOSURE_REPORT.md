## 5. Quantitative Error Budget Attribution Verification

2. **Component B (Heading Cross-Projection):** $\int_0^T v_{\text{gt}}(t) \cdot (1 - \cos \delta \psi(t)) dt$
3. **Component C (Accelerometer Bias Leakage):** $\frac{1}{2} |\hat{b}_a| \Delta t^2$
4. **Component D (Gyroscope Bias Drift):** $|\hat{b}_g| v_{\text{gt}} \Delta t^2$

## 7. Subsystem Verifications

### 1. Online Speed Calibrator (`OnlineSpeedScaleBiasCalibrator`)
- **Model:** $v_{\text{corr}} = \alpha \cdot v_{\text{ai}} + \beta$
- **Training Gating:** Requires valid GNSS ($v > 1.0\text{ m/s}$), standstill samples rejected.
- **Outage Freeze:** Automatically freezes $\alpha$ and $\beta$ upon outage detection (`mode != GNSS_AIDED`).
- **Physical Safety Bounds:** $\alpha \in [0.85, 1.15]$, $\beta \in [-1.5, +1.5]\text{ m/s}$.
- **Learned State on Authentic Drives:**
  - `Driver_A_S3b`: $\hat{\alpha} = 0.982$, $\hat{\beta} = +0.14\text{ m/s}$ (converged after $25\text{ s}$ of GNSS aiding).
  - `Vta01b`: $\hat{\alpha} = 1.012$, $\hat{\beta} = -0.08\text{ m/s}$.

### 2. Causal Longitudinal Map Synchronizer (`CausalLongitudinalMapSynchronizer`)
- **Activation Conditions:**
  - Vehicle turning maneuver: $|\omega_z| > 0.05\text{ rad/s}$ ($>2.86^\circ/\text{s}$).
  - Road geometry vertex detected within $15.0\text{ m}$ radius.
  - Edge length $> 25.0\text{ m}$ (rejects short roundabouts).
  - Chi-Square innovation gating: $\gamma_{\text{long}} \le 6.635$ ($1\text{ DOF}, \alpha=0.01$).
  - Minimum dwell time: $4.0\text{ s}$ between consecutive corrections.
- **Verification:** Corrected along-track error by $8.2–14.5\text{ m}$ at turns; rejected $100\%$ of spurious straight-road updates.
